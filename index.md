## Getting started

1. Open VisionDubber. The first time, it downloads its AI models (about 25 GB; the exact size is shown before the
   download starts). Keep the Mac on Wi-Fi and plugged in.
2. Click **New Dub** and choose a video. It is dubbed into English.
3. Confirm that you own the video or have permission from everyone whose voice is in it, then start. You can keep
   using the Mac; the dub continues in the background of the app.
4. When it finishes, play the dub, correct any line in the editor, and use **Export** for the video, the audio or
   the subtitles.

## Common questions

**Which Macs are supported?**
Macs with Apple silicon and 32 GB of memory or more, running macOS 26 or later. On a Mac with less memory this
version opens and says so: it downloads nothing, cannot dub, and VisionDubber Pro cannot be bought there.

**How long does a dub take?**
It depends on the Mac and the video. Settings shows the estimate for your Mac, and it follows your Mac's real
speed after its first dub. A one-minute clip is the quickest way to try it.

**How much disk space do I need?**
The models take about 25 GB. Each project takes about 1.5 GB for every 10 minutes of video. VisionDubber tells
you how much is missing before a dub starts. Delete a project in the app to get its space back.

**The model download failed.**
Check the connection and click **Retry**; files already downloaded are kept. You can move the models to another
disk in Settings → Models.

**A line is wrong, or the wrong person says it.**
Open the project, select the line, and edit its text or its speaker; VisionDubber voices that line again.

**Which languages can I dub into?**
English, American or British. The video can be in English or in 29 other languages: Arabic, Cantonese, Chinese,
Czech, Danish, Dutch, Filipino, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese,
Korean, Macedonian, Malay, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Turkish and
Vietnamese.

**Does my video leave my Mac?**
No. Everything runs on your Mac. See the [privacy policy](PrivacyPolicy).

**Are the dubs labelled as AI-generated?**
Yes. Exported videos and audio files with a dubbed voice say so in their metadata. Subtitles, scripts and
transcripts are plain text and carry no label.

**Whose voices may I dub?**
Only content you own, or for which you have permission from every person whose voice is used. Don't use
VisionDubber to imitate someone without their consent.

**What is free, and what does VisionDubber Pro add?**
Free: 10 minutes of dubbed video every calendar month, with every feature. A video counts when its dub finishes,
once for each language. Editing lines, new takes, dubbing the same video again and every export are free.
VisionDubber Pro removes the limit: monthly, yearly, or a one-time lifetime
purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

**I bought Pro on another Mac, or reinstalled VisionDubber.**
Settings → Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings → Subscriptions → VisionDubber Pro. Cancel at least
24 hours before the renewal date. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

**Where are the licences of the AI models?**
Settings → Acknowledgements.
