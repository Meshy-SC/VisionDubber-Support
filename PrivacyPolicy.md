# Privacy Policy for VisionDubber

**Last updated:** 23 November 2026

VisionDubber is a dubbing app for the Mac, made by Meshy SC. It dubs your videos into other languages on your Mac.
Your videos, the voices in them and the dubs never leave your Mac.

## What we collect

Nothing. VisionDubber has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. The developer never receives
your videos, your audio, your text or any information about how you use the app.

## What stays on your Mac

To work, VisionDubber keeps the following **only on your Mac**:

- **Your projects**: for each video you dub, a project with the separated sound, the transcript, the translation,
  the generated voices and the finished dub. A project is kept in the app's own storage until you delete it.
  VisionDubber reads the video you choose and writes exported files only where you choose.
- **The AI models**: downloaded once and stored in the app's own storage, or in a folder you choose in Settings.
- **Your settings.**

Every step runs on your Mac: separating voices from music, recognising speech, telling speakers apart,
translating, and generating the dubbed voices. Nothing is sent to us or to any AI service.

## Voices

To dub a speaker, VisionDubber analyses that speaker's voice in your video and generates the translated lines in a
similar voice. This analysis happens on your Mac, is stored only in the project, and is never sent anywhere. You
are asked to confirm, before each dub, that you own the content or have permission from everyone whose voice is
used. Delete the project to delete everything derived from those voices.

Exported videos and audio files with a dubbed voice carry a label in their metadata saying the voice is
AI-generated.

## Permissions

VisionDubber asks for no microphone, camera, contacts, location or screen access. It can open only the files and
folders you choose.

## Purchases

VisionDubber Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer never sees your payment
details or your Apple Account. VisionDubber only asks the App Store, on your Mac, whether Pro has been bought.
The minutes dubbed in the free plan are counted on your Mac, in a small file in the app's storage and one item in
your login keychain ("VisionDubber: free dubbing minutes used"); the count is never sent anywhere. Apple's privacy
policy applies to purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Network use

VisionDubber connects to the internet only to check VisionDubber Pro with the App Store and to download its AI
models. In this version the models are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a model hosting service. It
is a plain file download: Hugging Face receives what any web server receives for a download, such as your IP
address and the name of the file, and nothing about you or your videos. Hugging Face's privacy policy:
https://huggingface.co/privacy

## Children

VisionDubber does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
